## Full-checkpoint image-to-text E2E on GPUs 5/6, 2026-09-28

The canonical two-stage deployment could not finish loading its Thinker on GPU 6 while other jobs held device memory. A one-stage image-to-text Thinker deployment then ran with TP=2 across GPUs 5/6 and 6 GiB per-rank CPU weight offload. The test process registered the existing Thinker topology alone and passed vLLM's supported offload argument through a process-local Omni config compatibility shim; neither the installed environment nor this PR's source was changed. Decoder mode was `FULL_DECODE_ONLY`, encoder budgets were 4900/9800, `max_num_seqs=2`, and prefix/processor caching was disabled.

| Input request | Eager E2E (s) | Graph E2E (s) | Outcome |

| Blue square | 16.018 | — | Eager identified blue square |

No service speedup is claimed. The failed process has not exited; it was not manually terminated. Paired E2E and batch-two token parity remain open.
